## About the game  (limit 1000 — 936)

```
Welcome to Boar Blin, the legendary stall where a boar sells chips in person since 1997!

You take your place behind the counter as a line of the strangest customers forms up: grannies, cops, aliens and even zombies. Each has their own order and their own patience — dawdle, and they'll walk off to the store next door.

Assemble every order exactly, count out change with real bills and earn fat tips for speed and precision. From the second shift customers start paying with big notes, and you must count the change by hand, coin by coin and bill by bill.

Once per shift comes a RUSH: a bus dumps a crowd, the line jams up and prices double. At day's end you pay the stall rent, which climbs with every shift. Can't cover it — the shop gets shut down.

Between shifts, pour your earnings into upgrades: a fat price tag, a TV in the line, a tip jar, a roadside banner and coffee under the till. How many shifts will your boar survive?
```

## How to play  (limit 1000 — 816)

```
Goal — survive as many shifts as you can and don't go broke on rent.

1. Customers walk up to the counter. Click a customer's card to select them (or keys Q, W, E, R).
2. Read their order — the flavor emoji and the amount. Click the right packs on the shelf (or keys 1-6): they drop onto the counter.
3. Assembled the exact order — hit SERVE (or Enter). Items turn green when you've got it right.
4. A customer pays with a bill — count out the change with coins and notes. Exact to the ruble — you get bigger tips.
5. Wrong order, short change or a tired customer — you lose nerves (a heart). Out of nerves — game over.
6. At the end of a shift pay the rent and spend your earnings on upgrades.

Controls: mouse or touch everywhere. Keys: 1-6 goods/bills, Q W E R pick customer, Enter serve, Backspace undo, M music.
```
